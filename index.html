Research and coaching rules

Run until the fit changes

Marion’s system combines option roots with quick, intermediate, and vertical answers. CU’s game report credited its downhill run game and line push in the 14–13 Georgia Tech win. For GO-GO, stay on Inside Zone or Jet Inside Zone while the box count and fit are favorable. One short gain alone tells you little.

Use both backs as threats

Marion describes both backs carrying, blocking, and receiving from the same backfield. A true two-back alignment and a two-tight-end surface create different stresses. Use Deuce Twins Over as DISCO’s separate heavy changeup with Campbell and Carter.

Use motion to make the defense declare

Track the overhang, force defender, and safety through motion. A defender traveling with the jet is a clue. Confirm whether the defense exchanges assignments or rotates behind him. CU defenders described preparing against those shifts, motions, and option looks.

Pair each call with its constraint

Wing X Off gives you a four-call sequence from one look. Touch Pass tests force. Inside Zone punishes the widening fit. Peek Jet Pop hits the window when the hook vacates. Boot Wheel comes after the safety drives and the boot edge is covered.

Use tempo after the next call is clear

Marion connects tempo with keeping the defense reacting to the front it is already in. For GO-GO, keep the personnel and call ready when the defense shows a repeatable answer. Give yourself time to fix the protection or process a new front.

Call the answer at the sticks

Second and short buys a shot only when the shot is earned. Third and medium against a pressure picture favors Snag Spacing or Slants according to the current set and who replaces the blitzer. Against a capped shell, keep the run or underneath answer available.

    Go-Go principles in DISCO

    Start downhill with the backfield picture held constant. Marion’s two-back alignment puts different threats on the same side of the quarterback. The second back can change the run fit, release as a receiver, or sell the first action. DISCO uses its wing and jet calls to teach that same sequence of pressure and counters.     Brennan Marion teaches same-side two-back looks and mesh-wheel concepts. DISCO applies his leverage and constraint principles with its installed sets. Marion’s Go-Go passing clinic

    Build the drive around three numbers: box count, perimeter leverage, and rushers versus blockers. Stay in the same set when the defense gives you a different answer. Use the jet series to make the edge wrong twice. If the overhang widens with motion, hit inside. If the safety drives downhill after seeing jet, take the wheel when the boot edge is accounted for.

    Pressure rule. Identify who can come free and who replaces him in coverage. Against a mug or overload, the quick game is the answer only if the replacement defender leaves the lane. Check the flat or snag against its own defender. Keep the HB in on a longer shot when the protection art calls for it.
